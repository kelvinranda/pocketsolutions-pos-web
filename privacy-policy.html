Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026

This policy explains how the Pockets ICT Solutions POS mobile app (Android package co.zw.pocketsolutions.pos) collects, uses, stores and shares data. The app is provided by Pockets ICT Solutions ("we", "us").

The app is a point-of-sale tool for businesses. It is used by staff of a business that has an account with us. Accounts are created by that business, and the business decides what is recorded in it. We hold that business data on the business's behalf.

Data the app collects

DataExamplesWhy it is used
Account detailsUsername, password, name, role, company and branchTo sign you in and show what your role allows
Sales and payment recordsProducts, quantities, prices, totals, currency, payment method and amount, payment reference, receipt number, date and time, cashierTo record sales, produce receipts and reports, and reconcile cash
Customer nameA name the cashier enters on a sale. If none is entered, the sale is recorded as "Cash Customer"To show who a sale was for, on the receipt and in the business's records
Business operationsTill sessions, refunds, stock counts and transfers, goods received, activity historyTo run the business's stock and till processes and keep an audit trail

We do not use this data for advertising, and the app contains no advertising or analytics tools. We do not sell personal data.

Phone permissions and features

  • Camera: used only when you choose to scan a product barcode. The app does not save or upload camera images, and does not record audio.
  • Photos: used only when an admin chooses a company logo. The logo stays on that phone.
  • Bluetooth (Nearby devices): used to find and connect to a receipt printer. Receipts are sent to the printer you choose.
  • Internet and network status: used to send and receive data with our servers, and to tell whether you are online or offline.

The app does not access your location, contacts, messages, call history or microphone.

Data stored on your phone

So the app can work with no signal, it keeps some data on the phone: the product list, exchange rates, sales waiting to be sent, held sales, and printer settings. Your sign-in session is kept in the phone's secure storage. For offline sign-in, the app keeps a one-way hash of your password rather than the password itself.

Uninstalling the app removes this data from the phone. Any sales not yet sent to our servers are lost, so please sync before uninstalling.

How data is shared

We share data only in these cases:

  • The business you work for: its authorised staff can see its records, including sales made under your account.
  • Tax authorities: where a business uses fiscal receipts, receipt details are sent to the Zimbabwe Revenue Authority (ZIMRA) as the law requires.
  • Service providers: companies that host and maintain our servers, who may process data only to provide that service.
  • Legal reasons: where the law, a court or a government authority requires it, or where needed to protect people or our systems.

Links to other services

The login screen offers Call and WhatsApp links. If you tap one, your phone opens that service, and its own privacy terms apply. The app can also open the Pockets web POS, which uses cookies to keep you signed in.

Security

The app connects to our servers using encrypted HTTPS connections. Passwords are protected with one-way hashing when they are set or used to sign in. Access to a business's data depends on the user's role. No system is completely secure, so please keep your password private and lock your phone.

How long data is kept

Business records are kept for as long as the business has an account with us, and afterwards for as long as needed to meet tax, accounting or legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, including Zimbabwe's Cyber and Data Protection Act, you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to how it is used.

To change or delete an account, contact your business's administrator or contact us using the details below. We may need to keep some sales and tax records where the law requires it. Any change to data held for a business may need that business's approval.

You can withdraw a phone permission at any time in your phone's settings. The related feature will then stop working.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
