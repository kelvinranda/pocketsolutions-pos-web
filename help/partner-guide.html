Pockets POS is a ZIMRA-ready point of sale for Zimbabwean retailers: a web back office, a browser selling screen and a Windows till that keeps selling when the internet is down. This guide is for partners who sell, demonstrate and set up the system.

01

The pitch in one minute

ZIMRA fiscalisation built in

Every sale is a signed fiscal invoice and every refund a credit note, with QR code and verification code on the receipt. Fiscal days open by themselves; managers close them with one button.

Never stops selling

The Windows till sells with no internet and catches up automatically. When ZIMRA is unreachable, receipts are signed on the spot and sent later.

One system, every branch

Stock, prices, users, customers and reports for all branches in one place, with each till tied to its branch.

Control over cash

Opening floats, cash in / out, counted vs expected cash, shortages and surpluses, refund approvals and an audit trail for every change.

02

Who it is for

  • Retail shops, supermarkets and hardware stores: barcode scanning, items sold by weight or length, sizes and colours, stock control and purchasing.
  • Bars, bottle stores and restaurants: fast tile selling by button group, multiple tills, cash control per cashier.
  • Businesses with several branches: stock transfers, branch reports and one head-office view.
  • Any VAT-registered business that must fiscalise with ZIMRA.

05

Why offline matters

Internet and power cuts are a fact of trading. Pockets POS is built so neither one costs a sale:

  • The Windows till keeps a full copy of products, prices, users and customers. Cashiers sign in and sell with no connection.
  • Each completed sale is written to the PC's disk immediately, so a power cut does not lose it.
  • Sales go up to the web system automatically when the connection returns. Head office sees them without anyone pressing a button.
  • ZIMRA receipts are signed on the till and sent when ZIMRA is reachable. The fiscal day stays within ZIMRA's rules.
06

Running a demo

  1. Start on the selling screen. Ring up three items: tap a tile, tap + again, then tap the number and type 12. Scan a barcode if you have a scanner.
  2. Take payment with Cash and show the change, then show the receipt with the ZIMRA QR code.
  3. Request a refund as the cashier, then approve it as a supervisor: it shows the control owners want.
  4. Open the ZIMRA Fiscal Day page: the day opened by itself; show hours left and the close button.
  5. Show the Management Dashboard and Z Report: today's sales by cashier, cash expected vs counted.
  6. Unplug the network on the Windows till and keep selling. Reconnect and show the sales arriving on the web.
07

What a customer needs

  • Web system: any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox) on a PC, tablet or phone.
  • Windows till: Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. No internet needed to sell; internet for syncing and ZIMRA.
  • Receipt printer: any Windows printer; 58 mm or 80 mm thermal printers (Epson, Xprinter, Rongta, Bixolon and compatibles) for fast ESC/POS printing and paper cut. USB or network.
  • Cash drawer: one that plugs into the receipt printer.
  • Barcode scanner: any USB scanner that types like a keyboard.
  • ZIMRA: a fiscal device registered on the ZIMRA FDMS portal for each branch (device ID, serial number, activation key).
  • Recommended: a small UPS for the till PC.
08

Setting up a new customer

  1. Pocket Solutions creates the company and its first Admin.
  2. Pocket Solutions sets the company's base currency and the currencies it may use (Platform › Currencies); the Admin or a manager adds the exchange rates (Finance › Exchange Rates).
  3. The Admin adds branches, users, tax rates and button groups.
  4. Import products from the Excel template, then enter opening stock.
  5. Register each branch's ZIMRA device (test first, then production).
  6. Install the Windows till on each counter PC and pair it with a code from Desktop Tills.
  7. Connect the receipt printer and cash drawer in Till Settings and print a test receipt.
  8. Train cashiers on the daily routine: open, sell, reconcile, close. Train supervisors to close the ZIMRA fiscal day every evening.
09

Common questions from customers

Does it work with ZIMRA fiscalisation?
It connects to ZIMRA's Fiscal Device Management System (FDMS) as a fiscal device for each branch, issues fiscal invoices and credit notes, and manages fiscal days as ZIMRA requires. Each device is registered and tested on the ZIMRA test environment before going live.
What happens when the internet goes down?
The Windows till keeps selling, printing and fiscalising. Everything syncs when the connection returns.
Can we have more than one till?
Yes, as many as you need per branch. Tills in one shop can also share sales and stock over the local network.
Where is our data kept?
On the web server for the whole business, and a working copy on each Windows till. Both are backed up automatically.
Can staff steal or hide sales?
Every sale is numbered and fiscalised, refunds need approval, cash is reconciled per session, and price, stock and user changes are logged.
How do updates work?
New versions of the Windows till are published centrally. Tills update with one click, from the internet or a USB stick.
